Opening files

PDF, HWP, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, mail, Markdown and images

Formats: PDF · HWP (.hwp .hwpx) · Word (.docx) · PowerPoint (.pptx) · Excel (.xlsx .xls .ods .csv) · mail (.eml) · Markdown (.md) · images (jpg png gif bmp webp). No Office or Hancom Office needed.

  • 🖱️ Click 📂 Open file at the top, or press F2.
  • 🗂️ Drag and drop a file onto the window.
  • 🕘 Reopen one from Recent files on the start screen. (desktop app)
  • 📁 Double-click a file in Windows Explorer and it opens in WZ Reader. (installed version)
  • 🔗 Use 📂 ▾ → Open from URL and paste a link to a document on the web. (If the site blocks cross-origin access, download the file and open it.)
🔐 A password-protected PDF asks for its password when it opens. See Passwords.
🐘 PDFs over 500 MB open too (up to about 2 GB): only the parts in view are read, so they open quickly — view only. (desktop app)

View modes

Switch the layout to match what you are doing
📄
Single page
Scroll one page at a time, top to bottom. The default.
📖
Two-page spread
Two pages side by side, like a book. A wide page gets a row of its own.
🗂️
Grid view
Every page at a glance. Click one to jump to it.
🎬
Fullscreen
Fill the whole screen — made for talks and presentations.
💡 Zoom: Ctrl + mouse wheel, or the + / – buttons. Fit width makes the text bigger and sharper.
🔎 Find: Ctrl+F searches the document, in every format.
🎬 Fullscreen: F5 starts from the first page, Alt+F5 from the page you are on.
📊

Word · PowerPoint · Excel

As they were written — tables, charts and pictures, without Office
📝Word
Pages break where Word breaks them, with headers, footers and bullets.
📽️PowerPoint
Slides look as they do in PowerPoint. Hidden slides are skipped in the slideshow.
📈Excel
Fonts, colours, borders, merged cells and number formats as written. Every row of a sheet with 200,000+ rows.

Word and PowerPoint get the same single · two-page · grid layouts, page counter and page list as a PDF.

🗒️Speaker notes
A deck with notes shows them in a yellow box under each slide. Toggle them with the notes button.
💬Slideshow captions
In the fullscreen slideshow the notes run along the bottom as captions. C or the corner button turns them off.
🎬Narrated video
A deck with notes saves as an MP4 with the film button: the notes read aloud, with subtitles a player can switch on and off. (desktop app)
💾 All three save as PDF with text you can still select and search.
✉️

Mail · Markdown

Messages and notes, read like documents
✉️Mail (.eml)
Sender, recipients and body, with attachments you can save or open. Korean mail (EUC-KR) comes out right.
🛡️Remote images blocked
Images from the web stay hidden until you ask, so opening a message does not tell the sender.
📝Markdown (.md)
Headings, tables, code and task lists, typeset — with a contents rail that follows your reading.
✏️Edit Markdown
Turn on the pencil at the top to edit the source. Ctrl+S saves the .md file.
💡 Mail and Markdown print, search, go fullscreen, read aloud and save as PDF too. Printed and saved as text, so they stay sharp and selectable.
🔊

Read aloud

A natural voice, reading on your PC — no internet needed

Press the 🔊 speaker button or S (or Alt+S) and the document is read from where you are, a sentence at a time. The sentence is highlighted and the view follows it.

🎙️Voice · speed
Pick one of ten voices and a speed, then Apply — the next sentence uses them.
⏮️Move around
Alt+← previous sentence, Alt+→ next, Alt+Space pause/resume.
📽️Presentations
A PowerPoint deck with speaker notes is read from its notes (the script). In fullscreen the slides turn as it reads.
📷Scanned documents
Run text recognition first, and the recognized text is what gets read.
📥 The first time, you are asked before the voice (383 MB) is downloaded. After that it works offline, and nothing you read leaves the computer. (desktop app)

Pages

Navigate and edit from the page list — add, delete and reorder are PDF only
🖱️Jump to a page
Click a thumbnail in the list on the left.
↕️Reorder
Drag thumbnails up or down. (edit mode)
➕Add pages
Insert a blank page, or pages from another PDF. (edit mode)
🗑️Delete pages
Select pages and press Delete. Changed your mind? Ctrl+Z. (edit mode)
📤Save selection
Select pages, right-click → Save selection, and just those pages become a new PDF. Works in view mode too.
✅ Select several: hold Ctrl or Shift while clicking.
💡 Stamps and signatures follow their page when pages are deleted or reordered.
🔍

Text recognition (OCR)

Read the text in scans and photos — Korean and English

When the text is a picture, nothing can be copied or searched. Recognize it and you can select, copy, search and read it aloud. It runs on your device, no internet needed.

📄This page
The text recognition button, or R (or Alt+R). Or click the page four times.
📚Whole document
Whole document from the ▾ menu beside the button, or R twice. Progress is shown; you can cancel.
✂️Copy a region
Hold Ctrl and drag over an area: just that part is recognized and copied to the clipboard.
💾Keep it in the PDF
Save as PDF afterwards and the text goes into the file, searchable in any PDF reader.
💡 A PDF that already has text needs no recognition — Ctrl+F finds it straight away.
🖊️

Stamps · signatures · watermarks

Marks that stay in the file — turn on edit mode with the pencil at the top
📮Stamps
Pick a preset from the stamp button, or upload an image of your own. Each click on a page stamps it.
🗂️My stamps
An uploaded stamp is cleaned up (white background removed, margins trimmed) and kept in My stamps, at the size you last gave it.
✍️Signatures
Click the signature button, draw with mouse or touch, and place it.
🔏Watermarks
Set the text, size, colour and angle, and every page gets a translucent watermark.
📋 The same stamp on every page: select it, Ctrl+C, pick pages in the page list, Ctrl+V — it lands in the same spot on all of them.
↩️ Undo: Ctrl+Z, redo Ctrl+Y. Delete a selected stamp or signature with Delete. ESC puts the stamp tool down.
💾 Marks go into the file when you save as PDF. Opening another document or closing with unsaved marks asks first.
🔐

Passwords

Open locked PDFs, and lock or unlock when you save
🔓Opening
A locked PDF asks for its password, and asks again if it was wrong.
🔒Lock · unlock
The padlock at the top decides what the next save does. It does not save by itself — Save as PDF carries it out.
💡 A document that arrived locked is saved locked unless you unlock it with the padlock first. The file name gets _locked or _unlocked.

On-screen markup

For emphasis — never saved to the file
🖌️1 Yellow highlighter
Drag to draw in translucent yellow. Press 1 again to stop.
⬜2 Red rectangle
Drag to frame an area. Press 2 again to stop.
🧹 Clear all: press ESC or the eraser button at the top. In fullscreen, a second ESC leaves fullscreen.
🎯

Presenter tools

Emphasis tools in fullscreen — shown on screen only, never saved
✒️P Pen
Draw freely by dragging.
🖍️H Highlighter
Paint over text with a thick translucent stroke.
▭R Rectangle
Frame the area you drag over.
➤A Arrow
Point at a spot with an arrow.
🔴L Laser pointer
A red dot that follows the mouse and leaves no trace.
🔎Z Spotlight zoom
Magnify around the mouse; the wheel sets the zoom.

Change colour and width while drawing.

🎨1–5 Colour
Red · green · blue · yellow · orange.
↔️[ ] Width
[ thinner, ] thicker.
↩️Ctrl+Z · E
Remove the last stroke · erase everything.
💡 Word, mail and Markdown in fullscreen scroll a screenful at a time; + / – size the text and 0 resets it. PowerPoint opens as a slideshow.
⎋ Two-step ESC: with marks or a tool on, the first ESC clears them; the next leaves fullscreen.

Saving

The save button = Save as PDF (Ctrl+S) · the ▾ menu beside it has the rest

Every format has the same three in the ▾ menu: Save original file, Save as PDF and Export as EXE. PDF, HWP and images add booklet, HTML and images.

🗎
Save original file
The file as it was opened, under another name — a deck stays .pptx, a message .eml.
📄
Save as PDF
A PDF with your stamps, signatures, watermarks and recognized text. Word, PowerPoint, Excel, mail and Markdown become PDFs whose text you can select.
📚
Save as booklet
The two-page view as a PDF, two pages per sheet — print it and fold it.
PDF · HWP · images
🌐
Save HTML
One HTML file that opens in any browser.
PDF · HWP · images
🖼️
Save images
Every page as a high-resolution PNG, in a ZIP.
PDF · HWP · images
📦
Export as EXE
A program (.exe) that carries the open document in its own format — the recipient just double-clicks it, nothing to install; a deck comes with its slideshow and notes. With stamps or signatures on it, it carries the PDF that includes them, and a locked PDF still asks for its password.
Desktop app (installed or portable)
🎬 A PowerPoint deck with notes can also be saved as a narrated video (MP4) with the film button. See Word · PowerPoint · Excel.
🖨️

Printing

What you see — stamps, signatures and watermarks included

Click Print or press Ctrl+P; a preview comes first. PowerPoint prints on slide-sized paper, Word at each page's own size, and mail and Markdown as text, so they stay sharp.
